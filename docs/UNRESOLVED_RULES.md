# Source and release limitations

The supplied source is `Warhammer_40K_11th_Edition_Core_Rules_Summary.docx`. Its P231 explicitly excludes the complete Rules Appendix and army/mission supplements. Its edition claim is recorded as supplied, not independently authenticated. No missing official rule or profile values are supplied from remembered editions.

## Additional material needed for official/advanced play

- Authorized faction datasheets, points, keywords, model geometry, permitted attachments, transport capacities, detachment/faction/psychic/wargear definitions, enhancements and current errata.
- Official mission pack, deployment, scoring schedules, actions, battle-size constraints and exact exceptions.
- Full Rules Appendix, especially special passing, target/allocation exceptions and timing interactions condensed out of the summary.
- Actual model/body geometry for FRAME, flying/aircraft, vertical movement, stable upper surfaces and three-dimensional visibility.

Without those inputs the app exposes only original infantry, original points/mission and supported flat-board geometry. Epic Challenge and Tank Shock are unavailable because those original units are neither CHARACTER nor MONSTER/VEHICLE. No transports, Leaders/Support, aircraft, official army builder or faction content is claimed.

## Explicit training assumptions and geometry limits

- Two original squads per player, 100 original points per squad, fixed default deployment, 44×30-inch board, two round markers, five rounds and 5 VP per controlled marker per round. Equal final VP is a draw. No early elimination victory.
- Each model has a circular 32 mm base. Ground-level counter silhouettes use sampled 2D visibility. Rectangular solid footprints conservatively block movement and sight; this is an agreed training geometry, not a reconstruction of complex infantry-through-ruins exceptions.
- Movement submission is atomic. Charge/fight compulsory approach checks use straight-line reachability; exact obstacle-aware shortest-path obligations are not certified.
- Units with no enemy in overrun reach may explicitly pass, with a training label in history. P120 mentions special passing without supplying its complete definition.
- Homogeneous non-character model allocation uses the defender's chosen order with wounded models first. Mixed CHARACTER/save/Toughness groups and Precision choices are not available without eligible profiles.
- Command tests resolve when Command ends, leaving an explicit window for Insane Bravery beforehand. Optional eligible dice can be auto-accepted or reviewed by the rolling player. No future outcomes are generated before review ends.
- Source-covered special abilities beyond the original weapons exist as tested engine support, not as official rosters. Source-specific ability eligibility must still be supplied before importing units that use them.

These limits are visible in the rules reference and documentation. The release satisfies the original training-profile acceptance loop; it must not be represented as complete official matched play.
